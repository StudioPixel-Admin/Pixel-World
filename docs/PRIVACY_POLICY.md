# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to any website, launcher, account system, store page,
analytics collection, or support workflow associated with Pixel World.

This document is a template for a private commercial launch and should be tailored
before public release. It is not legal advice.

## 1. Overview
Pixel World is a private game project. The project is designed to minimize the
collection of personal information from players and users. Where data is collected,
it will be used only for legitimate game, security, support, and operational
purposes.

## 2. Information we may collect
Depending on the launch setup, we may collect:
- crash reports and diagnostic information
- device and platform information
- game version information
- account or profile information if an online account is created
- support messages or bug reports
- optional analytics data for stability and performance improvements

We do not sell personal information.

## 3. Data use
Collected data may be used to:
- improve game stability
- monitor performance and bug severity
- support player issues
- maintain security and platform integrity
- comply with legal or contractual obligations

## 4. Data storage and protection
Personal data will be stored securely using commercially reasonable controls,
including access restrictions, encryption in transit where appropriate, and
limited retention periods. We will not retain data longer than necessary for the
stated purpose.

## 5. Cookies and analytics
If a website or launcher is used, cookies or analytics tools may be employed for
site operation, security, and product performance analysis. Users should be given
clear notice and controls where required by local law.

## 6. Third-party services
Any third-party game platform, analytics provider, account system, or storefront
may have its own privacy practices. The project owner is responsible for ensuring
that all such services are reviewed and their data-handling obligations are
understood.

## 7. User rights
Depending on the jurisdiction, users may have rights to:
- request access to their personal data
- ask for corrections or deletion
- restrict or object to certain processing
- withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis

## 8. Contact
Privacy inquiries should be directed to:
- privacy@pixelstudios.example

## 9. Updates
This privacy policy may be updated over time. Material changes should be reflected
in the version history and communicated to users before enforcement.
