# Legal and Commercial Launch Notes

This document is a starter legal and operational template for a private commercial
video game project. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed by counsel before
public launch, publishing, distribution, or any commercial agreement.

## Project
- Project name: Pixel World
- Owner: Pixel Studios
- Status: Private / pre-launch commercial project

## Core legal position
This project is proprietary software and media. All rights, title, and interest in
this project remain with Pixel Studios unless otherwise assigned in writing.

The repository and all associated assets are protected by copyright, trade secret,
trademark, and other intellectual property laws. The code is not open source and
is not intended for public redistribution or public use without a written license.

## Permitted use
Authorized internal use is permitted only for:
- development
- testing
- bug fixing
- pre-release review
- internal stakeholder review
- launch preparation

Any exception must be approved in writing by an authorized representative of Pixel
Studios.

## Prohibited use
The following are prohibited unless explicitly approved in writing:
- copying or cloning this repository
- public publication or distribution
- commercial use outside an executed agreement
- redistribution to third parties
- reverse engineering, decompilation, or disassembly
- asset extraction or reuse in unrelated projects
- public disclosure of source code or design documents
- posting to public repositories or code-sharing platforms

## Third-party content
This project may include third-party libraries, engines, SDKs, audio assets, fonts,
artwork, or tooling. Those components remain subject to their own licenses. The
project owner must keep a current list of such dependencies and ensure compliance
with their terms.

## Launch checklist
Before a public or commercial launch, complete the following:
- confirm the legal entity name and ownership structure
- sign contracts for any contractors or studio contributors
- confirm any IP assignment or work-for-hire terms
- verify asset ownership and licensing for all art, music, and code
- confirm the privacy policy and terms of service for any website or launcher
- review any storefront, platform, or publisher agreement
- confirm trademark registrations and branding protection
- verify age-rating and classification requirements for the target market
- maintain a third-party software inventory
- keep all internal launch data, credentials, URLs, and beta access private
- do not publish production metadata or private build data to public storefronts
- review the private launch guidance in [PRIVATE_LAUNCH_GUIDE.md](PRIVATE_LAUNCH_GUIDE.md)

## Internal policy language
The project should be treated as confidential information. Access should be limited
to personnel with a clear business need. Source code, art, design documents, and
internal materials should not be shared externally unless approved in writing.

## Final note
This is a foundation for internal governance and launch readiness. It does not
replace legal review or formal contracts.
